# Session: 2026-10-02 — Nitrate testing cost anecdote

## Objective
Document what a nitrate compliance test cost (dollars, time) 1985–2005 and what crossing 50% of the MCL implied, with citations.

## Output

## Key findings
- Pre-1993 (40 CFR 141.23(l), 1975 rule): GW CWS every 3 yrs, SW annually; no 50% trigger.
- Phase II (eff. 1993-01-01, 141.23(d)): GW annual, SW quarterly (reducible to annual if 4 quarters <50% MCL); GW >=5 mg/L -> quarterly for >=1 yr.
- Lab fee: Idaho state lab $13.50 (fee table 10-7-88, still in IAC 2005); Montana $10 (1982); commercial $5–25 (EPA/ERCo 1975); USGS NWQL internal $4.73 (FY1990).
- Labor: EPA arsenic EA (2000) assumes 1 operator hour/sample at $28 (May 1999 $).
- Working figure ~$55/sample (2005 $); trigger adds ~3 samples, 3 hrs, ~$165 per entry point per year.
