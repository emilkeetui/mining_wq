# Session: 2026-06-05 — 2SLS exogeneity Q&A

## Objective
Clarify the formal exogeneity requirement for 2SLS instruments.

## Summary
Answered conceptual question about IV exogeneity. Key points:
- Moment condition: E[Z'ε] = 0 (required for consistency)
- Conditional mean: E[ε | Z] = 0 (stronger; gives unbiasedness)
- Two components: exclusion restriction + independence of instrument from unobservables
- Applied to ARP instrument: sulfur × post95 must affect violations only via production, and pre-existing sulfur must be uncorrelated with CWS violation trends
