\textit{Notes:} Dependent variable equals 1 if the utility had a violation of that type during the year, 0 otherwise; coefficients and standard errors are multiplied by 100 to show percentage point change. Upstream coal mines is the number of active coal mines in watersheds within two flow steps upstream of the utility's intake, summed across those watersheds. The two instruments interact an indicator for the post-1995 period with the average coal sulfur content of watersheds within two flow steps upstream and, separately, within two flow steps downstream of the utility's intake. The sample is utilities with a coal mine within two flow steps upstream of their intake and no coal mine colocated with their intake, in states with at least one other such utility. Standard errors clustered at the utility level. Upstream coal mines are instrumented with two instruments. The first-stage F-statistic (clustered at the utility level) tests the two instruments jointly. The overidentification test is Hansen's J statistic, robust to clustering at the utility level, for the null hypothesis that both instruments are valid. Sample period 1985--2005. Number of observations: 10,641; number of utilities: 565. *** p$<$0.01, ** p$<$0.05, * p$<$0.1.
