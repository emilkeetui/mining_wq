\textit{Notes:} Each row reports the coefficient on the number of active coal mines and the coefficient on coal production from separate regressions that differ only in the endogenous regressor. The first five panels report two-stage least squares estimates with utility and state $\times$ year fixed effects, where the regressor is summed over watersheds within two flow steps upstream of the utility's intake and instrumented with the interaction of a post-1995 indicator and average upstream coal sulfur content; production is annual. The last panel reports ordinary least squares estimates of mean measured concentration within two flow steps upstream with utility and HUC02 $\times$ year fixed effects, where production is annual. Standard errors clustered at the utility level in parentheses. The dependent variable and each regressor are converted to standard deviation units, so coefficients give the standard-deviation change in the outcome per one-standard-deviation change in the regressor. Ratio is the mines coefficient divided by the production coefficient; the within-SD ratio instead scales each regressor by its standard deviation after removing the fixed effects. The equality p-value tests that the two standardized coefficients are equal, using a joint regression clustered at the utility level. *** p$<$0.01, ** p$<$0.05, * p$<$0.1.
